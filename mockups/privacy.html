Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Overview

HomeAlert is a private home security and alert notification application. It is operated for personal use by authorized users only. This policy explains what information HomeAlert collects, how it is used, and your rights with respect to that information.

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal information to any third party, full stop.

Information We Collect

HomeAlert collects only the minimum information needed to operate:

  • Google account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo URL from Google's OAuth2 service. This is used solely to identify you within the application.
  • Security event data — HomeAlert records events triggered by connected devices (such as doorbell presses and motion detections), including the event type, device name, and timestamp. This data is used to populate your dashboard and deliver push notifications.
  • Push notification subscriptions — if you enable push notifications, your browser's push subscription endpoint and a label identifying your device (e.g. browser and operating system) are stored so that notifications can be delivered to you.
  • Notification preferences — your choices about which event types trigger push notifications are saved to your account.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to operate HomeAlert for you:

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To display your name and profile photo in the application
  • To show security event history on your dashboard
  • To send push notifications to your registered devices based on your preferences

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics platforms, or any purpose beyond operating the application.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with any third party. Your data is not used for advertising, is not shared with data brokers, and is not disclosed to any external service beyond what is strictly required to operate the application (i.e. Google's OAuth2 service for authentication and your browser's push notification service for delivering alerts).

Data Retention

Your account information and security event history are retained for as long as your account is active. Push notification subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe via the Settings screen or your browser revokes the subscription. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting the application administrator.

Security

HomeAlert is a private application accessible only to authorized accounts. Authentication is handled exclusively via Google OAuth2 — no passwords are stored. Sessions are secured using standard Rails session management. Push notification credentials are handled using the VAPID protocol and are never exposed to other users.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Unsubscribe from push notifications at any time via the Settings screen

To exercise any of these rights, contact the application administrator.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Continued use of HomeAlert after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
